Android Export Notes

  • Adaptive Icon:
    • Background layer: solid #FFFDF8
    • Foreground layer: Eko logo + "Sahayak Uploader" text stack, entirely within the 66dp safe zone of the 108dp canvas
    • Monochrome layer: Silhouette of logo + text, filled with system accent color
  • Play Store Icon: 512×512 full square (no rounding); Google Play applies its own mask
  • Small Sizes: Text illegible below 96px. Logo-only variant for future consideration.
  • Brand Color: --eko-amber: #FBB11B (pixel-identical to original logo, never substitute)
